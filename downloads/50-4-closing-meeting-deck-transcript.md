# 50.4 PUBLIC CLOSING MEETING TEMPLATE v0.1

Accessible text and presenter notes for the editable public-template deck. Every example is fictional; local routes and policy must be supplied by the adopter.

## Slide 1

- §50.4

Opening: replace the engagement label, owner, and date locally. This template does not authorize a meeting, publish findings, or set an SLA. The facilitator should use the actual report and applicable local workflow.

## Slide 2

Visible slide text:

- MEETING JOB
- What this meeting must settle
- Open Practice Framework
- 02
- 01 · What was tested?
- Confirm scope, dates, access, and meaningful limits. Do not let an untested area inherit the result.
- 02 · What was observed?
- Walk the owner through evidence and the exact consequence supported by it.
- 03 · Who owns the next move?
- Name the issue owner, technical support route, and any separate governance decision.
- 04 · What closes the loop?
- Agree the change/evidence/retest route and where the official record will live.

Presenter notes:

Ask the owner to restate the scope and immediate next action. If ownership or a governance route is unresolved, record that explicitly rather than filling it with an assumption.

This is a synthetic example, not a real vulnerability report. Point to the protected original request and response, explain the expected policy, and separate observed result from extrapolation.

## Slide 5

Visible slide text:

- EVIDENCE
- One packet, two jobs
- Open Practice Framework
- 05
- PROTECTED RECORD
- Originals, time/build/identity, reproduction conditions, reviewer disposition, custody and access controls.
- OWNER COPY
- Redacted proof, clear remediation direction, affected in-scope instances, and a retest condition.
- A screenshot helps orient. It does not replace a reproducible condition or a protected original.

Presenter notes:

Do not insert secrets, personal data, or restricted originals into a public slide. Establish the protected system of record and the recipient-safe copy. The adopting practice sets evidence retention and access rules.

## Slide 7

Visible slide text:

- RETEST
- Retest starts with readiness, not optimism
- Open Practice Framework
- 07
- ENVIRONMENT
- Named, reachable, and authorized for the retest.
- CHANGE
- The fix is deployed in that environment; state what changed.
- ACCESS
- Required test identities, roles, and data are available.
- EVIDENCE
- Owner evidence and expected pass condition are attached.
- The adopter chooses the request channel and service target; this template sets neither.

Presenter notes:

A missing condition is a readiness gap. Record it without converting it to a missed tester service target. The applicable local workflow determines timing and closure state.
