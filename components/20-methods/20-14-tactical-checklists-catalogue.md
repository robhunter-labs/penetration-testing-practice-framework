# 20.14 · Tactical Testing Checklists

> Framework component for adaptation. Publication does not authorize testing, certify conformance, or establish that an organization has adopted this practice.

**Catalogue-only entry — workbook intentionally not distributed.** This identifier is retained so the framework inventory is complete and the role of a tactical coverage workbook is visible. The framework's methods describe how to plan, test, interpret, and report; an adopter should maintain its own current checklists mapped to its scope, technology, applicable standards, and rules of engagement. A checklist is a coverage aid, not authorization to run a technique or proof that a control passed.

No §20.14 workbook is included in the public package. This is deliberate: a checklist needs to reflect the adopter's scope, methods, tools, and evidence rules. Review the relevant framework method and engagement controls, then use or build the workbook appropriate to your environment.
